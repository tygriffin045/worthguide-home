fifteen mugs, one for his breed.

Dog dads can be hard to shop for. Plenty of them say they don't need anything, but most of them will happily talk about their dog for twenty minutes. These fifteen coffee mugs lean into that. They come from KettleAndKibbleCo, a small Etsy shop that makes cartoon dog breed mugs, and it is part of the same small team behind this site. Each mug pairs one breed with one short line about life with that dog, so the easiest way to choose is to start with his dog.

Each button opens the mug's Etsy listing, where you can check the current price, the mug size and estimated delivery. Christmas shipping windows fill up in December, so it is worth checking the delivery estimate on the listing before you order.

Picking the right one for him

  • Match the breed first. A mug of his actual breed reads as thoughtful; a generic “dog dad” line can feel like a last-minute pick.
  • Think about where he drinks coffee. An 11oz mug suits a desk, a workshop bench or the porch on a cold morning.
  • Two dogs? Two mugs of the two breeds make a simple matching set.
  • Pair it up. A bag of his favorite coffee or a new toy for the dog turns a mug into a full gift.
